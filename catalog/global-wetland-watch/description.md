[Global Wetland Watch](https://www.globalwetlandwatch.org/) is an initiative
for mapping and monitoring wetland ecosystems, led by a partnership of
[DHI](https://www.dhigroup.com/), the
[UNEP-DHI Centre on Water and Environment](https://www.unepdhi.org/) and the
[United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP)](https://www.unep.org/).

Its products are designed to be used flexibly, so that countries can adapt them to
their reporting and decision-making under multilateral environmental agreements, including the 2030
Agenda for Sustainable Development, the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity
Framework and the Ramsar Convention on Wetlands.
