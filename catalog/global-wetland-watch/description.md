Global Wetland Watch produces global, Earth observation based maps of
wetland extent and wetland ecosystem type, together with the hydrological and
coastal input layers used to derive them. The products are generated in Earth
Engine from Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, ALOS-2 PALSAR-2 (Advanced Land Observing Satellite 2, Phased Array
type L-band Synthetic Aperture Radar 2) and terrain data, and cover
the period 2017&ndash;2025.

Global Wetland Watch is a partnership of
[DHI](https://www.dhigroup.com/),
[UNEP-DHI Centre on Water and Environment](https://www.unepdhi.org/) and the
[United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP)](https://www.unep.org/). More information is available at
[globalwetlandwatch.org](https://www.globalwetlandwatch.org/).
