
The Global Ecosystem Dynamics Investigation [GEDI](https://gedi.umd.edu/)
mission aims to characterize ecosystem structure and dynamics to enable
radically improved quantification and understanding of the Earth's carbon cycle
and biodiversity. The GEDI instrument, attached to the International Space
Station (ISS), collects data globally between 51.6&deg; N and 51.6&deg; S
latitudes at the highest resolution and densest sampling of the 3-dimensional
structure of the Earth. The GEDI instrument consists of three lasers producing
a total of eight beam ground transects, which instantaneously sample eight
~25 m footprints spaced approximately every 60 m along-track. No data were
acquired during the ISS hibernation period from March 17, 2023, to April 24,
2024; operations resumed on April 26, 2024.
